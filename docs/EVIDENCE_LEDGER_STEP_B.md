## Current Research Consequence

B8/B9 established that the known stale class generalizes across tested held-Use2 attacks and that destructive AISetState occurs after the native cleanup opportunity has been lost on the bad path, while clean/reaction paths fulfill cleanup before that point. C1 then validated the consequence-based per-source obligation model in shadow form, including inherited `7 -> 7`, but exposed that CombatMove begins too late to acquire every legitimate execution.

C1-O1 closes the outer-lifetime identity gate strongly enough to proceed without another broad identity probe:

```text
outer attack ScriptFunction already live
→ legitimate equipped-weapon offense may occur before CombatMove
→ same live frame can persist into later CombatMove and cleanup
→ destructive AISetState can remove that frame without cleanup
→ raw arguments/frame addresses may be reused only after retirement
```

The higher-level architectural gate remains **C1-O2 shadow outer ScriptFunction binding integration**, but direct dispatch capture is currently suspended after hook-path crashes. `SESSION_ENTRYPOINT.md` owns the exact immediate hardening responsibility and route back toward that gate. Cleanup remains source/consequence based and existing AISetState `WOULD_REPAIR` shadow finalization remains unchanged.

Production physical repair remains disabled. `RunScriptFunction` return, FullStop and AISetState are not unconditional cleanup authorities. No family/action/input classifier, arbitrary group-7 adoption, polling/timers/scans, null-arguments fallback, or marker-core simplification is authorized by this evidence.
