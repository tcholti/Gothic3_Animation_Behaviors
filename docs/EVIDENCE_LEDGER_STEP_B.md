B7b resolves the causal chain for the tested player full-Whirl held-Use2 failure:

```text

→ Use2 remains held beyond 2500 ms
→ Script_Game +0x633F1 calls PSRoutine::FullStop()
→ active sAICombatMoveInstr receives fullStop=true and terminates
```
