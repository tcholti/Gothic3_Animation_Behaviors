## Current Research Consequence

The universal-guard architecture remains unchanged. `RunScriptFunction` is still recursion-safe pure pass-through; AISetState and AIFullStop remain validated explicit-this `.ThisCall()` member hooks; C1 physical repair is disabled; the known bad held-Use2 path remains a destructive stress case rather than production authority.

EV-201 changes the transport prerequisite state materially: the isolated SetCollisionGroup explicit-this conversion is **not runtime-stable** and failed its extended gameplay gate. The run simultaneously strengthened the shadow-guard model: repeated clean/native-cleanup, pre-activation, armed destructive abandonment, inherited-stale and source-specific controls continued to classify as expected, including 23 real `WOULD_REPAIR` outcomes and only the known seven unowned pre-CombatMove/GetUpAttack warnings.

The crash is localized close to the SetCollisionGroup wrapper's pre-call collision-group observation path, but the exact member-hook transport mechanism is not yet proven. Therefore do not reinterpret this as a failure of the C1 obligation model itself, and do not use it to justify physical repair or C1-O2 resumption.

The next responsibility is a **narrow SetCollisionGroup transport reassessment** using the tested wrapper/crash/disassembly evidence. Prefer the smallest transport correction that restores a proven stable baseline while preserving all existing collision/lifecycle semantics. StartEffect, PlayMotion and StopMotion remain outside this decision unless new direct evidence makes one of them relevant.
