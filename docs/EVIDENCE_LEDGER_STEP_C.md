## Current Research Consequence

The universal-guard architecture remains unchanged. `RunScriptFunction` is still recursion-safe pure pass-through; AISetState and AIFullStop are now validated explicit-this `.ThisCall()` member hooks; C1 physical repair is disabled; the known bad held-Use2 path remains a destructive stress case rather than production authority.

The original C1-O2 registered-ScriptFunction crash is **not explained or closed** by EV-199/EV-200. Current source still contains legacy ThisCall transport for StartEffect, SetCollisionGroup, PlayMotion and StopMotion. Do not harden them mechanically as a list.

`SetCollisionGroup` is the leading next prerequisite to inspect because it remains a shared-`GetSelf<eCEntity *>()` member hook on the exact path by which C1 observes both successful `Item_Attack` requests (including inherited `7 -> 7`) and native cleanup fulfillment. If its SDK/hook contract supports an isolated explicit-this `.ThisCall()` conversion with no ordering or collision/lifecycle semantic change, validate that transport alone and then reassess C1-O2 directly rather than automatically converting unrelated visual/marker hooks.
