The universal-guard architecture remains unchanged. `RunScriptFunction` is still recursion-safe pure pass-through. AISetState, AIFullStop and SetCollisionGroup now each have tested explicit-this `.ThisCall()` stability evidence. The C1 finalizer source-liveness correction has also passed isolated and extended stability gates. Physical repair remains disabled.

EV-201 remains the historical failed extended run, while EV-202 identifies its independent finalizer-lifetime crash and EV-203 establishes the corrected rerun. SetCollisionGroup is therefore no longer merely “not shown to have failed”; it is **PASS for tested extended gameplay stability** under the corrected finalizer substrate.

C1-O2 remains the unresolved higher-level destination because GetUpAttack can still legitimately request offense before CombatMove and the original direct `RunScriptFunction` dispatch-capture attempt still has its own registered-ScriptFunction crash history. The next responsibility is not implementation by default. Normal Chat must first reassess EV-199 against the now-stabilized substrate and choose the smallest falsifiable source/diagnostic candidate. Do not automatically restore the failed capture, harden unrelated hooks, or enable physical repair.
