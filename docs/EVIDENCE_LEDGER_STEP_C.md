## Current Research Consequence

The universal-guard architecture remains unchanged and physical repair remains disabled. AISetState, AIFullStop and SetCollisionGroup have tested explicit-this `.ThisCall()` stability evidence; the C1 finalizer source-liveness correction has passed isolated and extended stability gates.

P1 now establishes a narrower safe `RunScriptFunction` substrate than the rejected eager capture: stack-local/TLS current scope only, with expensive/live-frame work deferred until a real relevant offense. P1-B and P1-C passed their isolated and targeted responsibilities; no separate long-duration P1-D claim is made.

The current C1-O2 question is therefore no longer whether to restore general dispatch capture. C1-O2-P2 must test whether a legitimate pre-Combat offense can lazily create C1's existing monotonic generation from the proven live scope, whether the matching CombatMove in that same live invocation reuses the generation, and whether the temporary native binding can be consumed before the wrapper returns. If that smaller model works, cross-suspension native-frame ownership and continuation-return matching are unnecessary for the proven GetUp path and should not be added merely because the earlier design anticipated them.
